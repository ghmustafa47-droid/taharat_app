# Taharah mobile demo — original discussion cross-check

Source: the original product strategy review, with the two user-supplied screenshots clarifying the five-step module and block-level targeting examples.

| Original expectation | Mobile demo decision |
|---|---|
| Beginner “Understanding Wudu” and manuscript page 1 transformation | Separate five-step module, with progress, Previous/Continue, same-step language switching, and a route into the practical guide. Module steps are learning beats, not a religious sequence. |
| Illustrated Wudu guide, pages 13–16 | Retain the scrollable guide and its separate physical-method sections. Context art and stage icons are not reviewed action demonstrations. |
| Video block | Demonstrate poster, video availability, and transcript preview. Actual media, captions, and approved demonstration remain deliverables. The client presentation video is a separate artifact. |
| Verified source library | Show named citation, manuscript provenance, and review state as separate fields. Do not display an approved badge for an unreviewed manuscript transcription, translation, or interpretation. |
| Lesson eligibility and block visibility | Keep separate concepts. The beginner module is available from all four learning paths. The shared guide currently showcases multiple activities; this is not proof of runtime block filtering. |
| Three transformations | Existing library maps all 29 pages; pages 10–12 have a category route and pages 19–22 have a situation route. These require deeper interactive treatment and approved copy to match the Wudu demonstration. |
| Independent language, audience, and perspective | Language controls remain separate. Perspective selection and an unavailable perspective state remain distinct design work. |
| Original → transcription → educational adaptation | Page provenance exists in the mobile design; the full source/transcription comparison belongs in the private showcase and requires verified transcription. |

## Follow-up navigation and content pass

- All 68 learner frames and overlays are reachable from the English entry in the Figma action graph. The audit counted 302 actions, no missing destinations, and no targets below 44 × 44. Full learner screens have a language-appropriate Home route; overlays have Close actions. The typography comparison board is design-review material outside this graph.
- The five beginner steps now paraphrase visible material on manuscript page 1 (SRC-001): the prayer preparation passage, washing, wiping, the narration attributed on the page to Sahih al-Bukhari 135, and a recall prompt. Source identification is separate from scholarly verification.
- The video block now shows a poster, central play control, scrub track, time label and caption indicator. It opens the storyboard/transcript preview; there is no approved clip to play.
- Still missing from the original recommendation: actual audience filtering within lessons, reviewed action-by-action illustrations and video, verified Arabic/translation reference records, and an explicit scholarly-perspective selection/unavailable state. The rules and everyday-situations routes remain lighter than the Wudu journey. Test the Figma player on representative iOS and Android devices before the client walkthrough.
