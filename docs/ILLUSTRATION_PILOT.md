## Shared art direction

Use layered matte paper, rounded hand-cut shapes, fine paper grain and soft shadows between layers. Use warm ivory (#F7F1E7), deep teal (#174843), muted sage (#A9BBA7), soft terracotta (#BD735B) and pale blue for water. Keep the focal subject within the middle 75% of the frame so it survives mobile cropping. Welcome scenes can use decorative foliage and architectural silhouettes from I01. For instructional close-ups, use a plain ivory ground and keep texture subtle so hands and water remain legible. Leave titles, labels and captions to the app so English and Urdu can sit above or below the same artwork. Keep each illustration free of lettering, numbers, logos and UI.

## Nano Banana prompts

Prepend the shared art direction to each prompt. For I02, attach the selected I01 artwork as a style and character reference.

### I01 · Welcome to Taharah

Create one landscape 4:3 editorial illustration for the home screen of a bilingual Islamic learning app. A curious South Asian child around nine, with warm brown skin, short dark hair and a sage long-sleeved shirt, stands beside a simple ceramic basin. The child holds a folded ivory towel at waist level. A small blue water ripple in the basin provides a quiet visual accent. Use three-quarter framing with the child and basin centered as one composition. Give the child a relaxed, attentive expression and natural proportions. Keep the setting uncluttered and the tap off. The scene should feel welcoming to children and adults.

### I02 · Learning together

Create one landscape 4:3 editorial illustration using the attached I01 image as the character and style reference. Show the same child sitting beside a parent at a small table, both looking at a simple open picture booklet. The parent wears muted terracotta and gently points toward an abstract blue water droplet printed on one page. The child leans forward with interest. Frame both faces and the booklet in a centered composition. Give the pointing hand a natural pose and keep the booklet text-free. Use the same palette, line treatment and warm ivory background as the reference.

### I03 · Hands and water composition study

Create one square editorial illustration that tests the framing for a future instructional image. Show a close crop of two relaxed, clearly separated hands held above a shallow ceramic basin with a narrow stream of water visible between them. Include wrists and enough forearm to make the orientation legible. Use a three-quarter view, a simple background, and restrained blue highlights so water reads clearly at phone size. Preserve natural anatomy and keep hands, water and basin within the crop-safe area. Omit motion arrows, sequence marks and step cues.

## Pilot review

At roughly 320 px wide, check that each focal subject reads quickly and that cropping preserves faces and hands. Place English and Urdu captions outside the art to test the same image in both layouts. Compare the child across I01 and I02 for consistent proportions, clothing and palette. For the final I03 prompt, replace the composition study with an approved description of the exact action, including which hand moves, the contact area and where the water flows. Record the manuscript source and review result with the finished image.
